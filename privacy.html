Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

The short version

FirstDate does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking in the app.

What happens in the app

The cards are built into the app, and what you answer or do during the game stays between you and your date. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

In-app purchases

Level 5 is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple. The app asks Apple whether the purchase has been made so it can unlock the level. We don't receive your name, your payment details or your Apple ID. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Age limit

FirstDate contains adult themes and is intended for people aged 18 and over.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses when pages are visited, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Your rights

Because we don't hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have questions, or contact us by email, we use your message only to answer you and delete it when it's no longer needed.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

Personvernerklæring

FirstDate samler ikke inn, lagrer eller deler personopplysninger. Appen har ingen kontoer, analyseverktøy, annonser eller sporing. Kortene ligger i appen, og det du svarer eller gjør under spillet sendes ikke til oss.

Nivå 5 er et engangskjøp som håndteres av Apple. Appen spør Apple om kjøpet er gjort for å kunne låse opp nivået. Vi får ikke navnet ditt, betalingsopplysningene dine eller Apple-ID-en din.

FirstDate har voksent innhold og er ment for personer over 18 år. Siden er lagret hos GitHub Pages, og GitHub kan logge tekniske opplysninger som IP-adresse ved besøk.

Siden vi ikke har personopplysninger om deg, har vi heller ingenting å vise frem, rette eller slette. Hvis du kontakter oss på e-post, bruker vi meldingen bare til å svare deg, og sletter den når vi ikke lenger trenger den.
