Common questions

I bought Level 5, but it's locked

Open Level 5 and tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

Is it a subscription?

No. Level 5 is a one-time purchase. Levels 1 to 4 are free.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
